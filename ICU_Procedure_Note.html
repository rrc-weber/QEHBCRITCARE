ICU procedure note

Choose a procedure, fill in the applicable information, then copy the text into the relevant procedure note on PICS. Fields you leave blank are left out of the note.

Text only. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere. Do not type patient names, NHS numbers or dates of birth. Record the text in PICS via the correct patient record > Procedures > Performed > select procedure. It is essential that all invasive procedures are recorded accurately.
